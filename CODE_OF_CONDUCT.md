# Code of conduct

ReliaForge contributors are expected to keep collaboration respectful, constructive, and focused on
the work.

- Be considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction in good faith.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior makes
the community unsafe or unproductive. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository
owner's GitHub profile.
